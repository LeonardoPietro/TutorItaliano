# Stage 11 — Fluency & Expert Polish

**Theme:** Authentic texts/news, nuanced idiom, regional variation, sustained conversation.
**Status:** not_started

## Objectives
The final climb from strong intermediate to expert: real, unadapted Italian,
sustained unscripted conversation, and confident register/nuance control.

## Topic checklist
- [ ] Reading authentic short news articles, extracting meaning without translation
- [ ] Regional variation awareness (major dialect/accent differences — awareness, not mastery)
- [ ] Advanced idiom and colloquialism
- [ ] Sustained unscripted conversation strategies (fillers, repair phrases, clarification requests)
- [ ] Nuance: tone and register-shifting mid-conversation
- [ ] Writing a short structured essay/opinion piece
- [ ] Self-directed listening comprehension (podcasts/media, no subtitles)

## Milestone
Discuss a real Italian news article aloud, unscripted, for 10 minutes.

## Definition of done
- All topics taught, checked, and logged.
- Milestone artifact (recording/transcript + notes) exists in `playground/` or `exercises/solved/`.
- Stage assessment (§10) scored ≥80%.
- Flashcards created only for genuine gaps surfaced during authentic material — by this stage, most review should be consolidation.
