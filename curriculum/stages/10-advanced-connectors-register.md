# Stage 10 — Advanced Connectors & Register

**Theme:** Relative pronouns, passive voice, formal vs. informal register, idioms.
**Status:** not_started

## Objectives
Round out grammar with the connective tissue of longer, more sophisticated
speech and writing, and teach the learner to shift formality deliberately.

## Topic checklist
- [ ] Relative pronouns (che, cui, il quale)
- [ ] Passive voice (essere + participio; si passivante)
- [ ] Formal register (Lei form, formal conjugation, formal vocabulary)
- [ ] Formal vs. informal email/letter conventions
- [ ] Common idiomatic expressions & fixed phrases
- [ ] Argumentation connectors (tuttavia, quindi, inoltre, dato che)
- [ ] Register-shifting the same content between formal and informal

## Milestone
Write a formal email in Italian, then retell the same content informally to
a friend.

## Definition of done
- All topics taught, checked, and logged.
- Milestone artifact exists in `playground/` or `exercises/solved/`.
- Stage assessment (§10) scored ≥80%.
- Flashcards created for connectors and formal/informal vocabulary pairs.
