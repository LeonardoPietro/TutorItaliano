# Stage 04 — Past I: Passato Prossimo

**Theme:** avere/essere auxiliary choice, past participles, time expressions.
**Status:** not_started

## Objectives
Give the learner their first past tense so they can talk about anything that
already happened — the single most-used past form in spoken Italian.

> **Placement note:** the learner made a correctly-shaped but low-confidence
> attempt at this during onboarding (*"ho mangiato"*). Treat as reinforcement
> of a half-formed instinct, not first exposure — confirm the good instinct
> quickly, then fill the actual gaps (auxiliary choice, agreement, irregulars).

## Topic checklist
- [ ] Passato prossimo formation with **avere**
- [ ] Passato prossimo formation with **essere** (motion/state verbs)
- [ ] Past participle agreement with essere subjects (gender/number)
- [ ] Irregular past participles (fatto, detto, visto, preso, letto, ecc.)
- [ ] Time expressions for the past (ieri, la settimana scorsa, ... fa)
- [ ] Reflexive verbs in the passato prossimo
- [ ] Negation in the passato prossimo
- [ ] Sequencing a narrative (prima, poi, dopo, infine)

## Milestone
Tell the story of yesterday in 10+ sentences, past tense throughout, unaided.

## Definition of done
- All topics taught, checked, and logged.
- Milestone artifact exists in `playground/` or `exercises/solved/`.
- Stage assessment (§10) scored ≥80%.
- Flashcards created for irregular participles and the avere/essere split.
