# Stage 07 — Future & Conditional

**Theme:** Future tense, conditional mood, expressing plans, polite requests.
**Status:** not_started

## Objectives
Give the learner tenses for what hasn't happened yet and for politeness —
critical for travel logistics and for sounding courteous rather than blunt.

## Topic checklist
- [ ] Futuro semplice formation (regular + irregular stems)
- [ ] Futuro for predictions and plans
- [ ] Futuro of probability (guessing/supposition — "sarà," "avrà")
- [ ] Conditional presente formation
- [ ] Conditional for polite requests (vorrei, potrebbe, mi piacerebbe)
- [ ] Conditional for soft hypothetical statements
- [ ] Conditional passato, recognition-level ("would have")
- [ ] Sequencing plans (prima di + infinito, dopo aver + participio)

## Milestone
Plan a trip to Italy in Italian and make 3 politely-phrased requests, unaided.

## Definition of done
- All topics taught, checked, and logged.
- Milestone artifact exists in `playground/` or `exercises/solved/`.
- Stage assessment (§10) scored ≥80%.
- Flashcards created for irregular future/conditional stems.
