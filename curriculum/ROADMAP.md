# Italian — Beginner → Expert Roadmap

**Learner goal:** Travel-conversational Italian — handle everyday situations
confidently (ordering food, directions, small talk, hotels) — with the road
continuing on to full fluency for anyone who wants to keep climbing.
**Time budget:** 3–5 hours/week.
**Started:** 2026-07-21.

**Placement notes (from onboarding diagnostic):** true beginner for grammar
purposes. Already comfortable with a handful of fixed survival phrases
(*buongiorno*, *vorrei un caffè*, *sono stanco*) — these will not be re-taught
from scratch, just logged as known. Made a correctly-shaped but low-confidence
attempt at passato prossimo (*ho mangiato*) — flag for reinforcement, not
first-exposure teaching, when Stage 03 arrives. No working knowledge yet of
noun/article gender agreement, conditional, or subjunctive (confirmed by a
mistranslated hypothetical during placement) — these start from zero.

Stages are ordered by grammatical/functional dependency. Early stages are
weighted toward real travel situations (the stated goal); later stages extend
into full expert command of the language for anyone continuing past the
original goal.

---

## Stages

| # | Stage | Theme | Milestone |
|---|-------|-------|-----------|
| 00 | Foundations | Sounds, alphabet, gender/number, articles, essere & avere | Introduce yourself + describe 5 objects with correct articles |
| 01 | Prepositions | Simple prepositions + articulated (contracted) forms with definite articles | Describe the location of 5 objects/places using 4+ articulated prepositions |
| 02 | Present-Tense Basics | Regular verb families + core irregulars, adjectives, questions | Describe your daily routine in 8+ correct sentences |
| 03 | Travel Survival | Food, directions, shopping, time/dates, transport | Full role-play: order a meal AND ask/follow directions across town |
| 04 | Past I — Passato Prossimo | avere/essere auxiliary choice, past participles, time expressions | Tell the story of yesterday in 10+ sentences, past tense throughout |
| 05 | Past II — Imperfetto & Description | imperfetto vs. passato prossimo, physical/personality description | Describe your childhood and compare it to now |
| 06 | Pronouns & Connected Speech | Direct/indirect object pronouns, combined pronouns, ne/ci, reflexives | Retell a story using pronouns instead of repeating nouns |
| 07 | Future & Conditional | Future tense, conditional mood, polite requests, plans | Plan a trip to Italy and politely request 3 things |
| 08 | Subjunctive I | congiuntivo presente, opinions/doubts/wishes and their triggers | Give 5 opinions using "penso che", "è possibile che", etc. |
| 09 | Subjunctive II & Hypotheticals | congiuntivo imperfetto/passato, periodo ipotetico ("if" sentences) | Correctly build 5 hypothetical sentences (2nd & 3rd conditional) |
| 10 | Advanced Connectors & Register | Relative pronouns, passive voice, formal vs. informal register, idioms | Write a formal email AND retell the same content informally |
| 11 | Fluency & Expert Polish | Authentic texts/news, nuanced idiom, regional variation, sustained conversation | Discuss a real Italian news article aloud for 10 minutes, unscripted |

Each stage has a detail file in `curriculum/stages/NN-name.md` with its full
topic checklist and definition of done. A stage is complete when its
assessment (§10 of the operating instructions) scores ≥80% **and** its
milestone artifact exists in `playground/` or `exercises/solved/`.
