## Session focus: Stage 00 topic — Plural patterns (singular→plural)

### WHY
Noun gender was already mastered, but all vocabulary so far was singular-only. Plural forms are needed to say things like "os livros" / "as chaves," and the plural ending depends on the same -o/-a/-e pattern already learned.

### EXPLAIN
Three rules taught:
1. -O → -I (regardless of gender pattern established earlier): libro → libri
2. -A → -E: casa → case
3. -E → -I, independent of gender (masc. or fem.): fiore → fiori, notte → notti

### SHOW / CHECK / PRACTICE
Four unaided predictions, all correct on first try:
- libro → **libri** ✅
- chiave → **chiavi** ✅
- casa → **case** ✅
- notte → **notti** ✅

### REFLECT
Asked why "fiore" (masc.) and "notte" (fem.) share the same plural ending (-i) despite opposite genders.
Learner answer: "porque os dois terminam em E" — correctly identified that plural formation keys off the final letter, not grammatical gender.
**No re-teaching needed** — conceptual understanding confirmed, not just pattern memorization.
