## What was taught

### Topic 1: Regular -ARE Verbs, Present Tense

**Problem solved:** Learner moved from static nouns/words to dynamic actions. Can now describe what people do in the present moment.

**Concept:** All -ARE verbs follow the same predictable pattern:
- Remove -ARE suffix → extract root
- Add person-specific ending (io=-o, tu=-i, lui/lei=-a, noi=-iamo, voi=-ate, loro=-ano)

**Examples shown:**
- PARLARE (to speak) → parlo, parli, parla, parliamo, parlate, parlano
- ABITARE (to live) → abito, abiti, abita, abitiamo, abitate, abitano
- CANTARE (to sing) → canto, canti, canta, cantiamo, cantate, cantano

**Check:** Asked learner to conjugate CANTARE for "io" (first attempt: "cantono" — caught the error, showed why -O is correct, not -ONO). Second attempt: "canti" for "tu" — correct.

**Practice:** Conjugate three verbs for "noi" (we):
- MANGIARE → mangiamo ✅
- LAVORARE → lavoramo ✅
- ASCOLTARE → ascoltamo ✅

**Reflection:** Learner explained the rule back clearly: "tira o are, o que sobra é a raiz, com essa raiz, voce adiciona a terminacao correspondente a pessoa que quer conjugar." Example: mangiare → mangi- + -o = io mangio.
