## Session focus: Stage 00 topic — Definite articles (feminine singular + all plurals)

### WHY
Masculine singular (il/lo/l') was already solid. But half of previously-learned nouns are feminine (e.g., "casa"), and none of the plural forms had been taught yet — both are needed to form complete sentences in any gender/number.

### EXPLAIN
1. **la** — default rule for feminine words: la casa, la chiave
2. **l'** — before a vowel (same logic as masculine l'): l'amica, l'ora
3. Plural of **il** → **i**: il treno → i treni
4. Plural of **lo/l'** (masc.) → **gli** (inherits both triggers): lo studente → gli studenti; l'amico → gli amici
5. Plural of **la/l'** (fem.) → **le**, always, regardless of starting letter: la casa → le case; l'amica → le amiche

### SHOW / CHECK
Four unaided checks, all correct on first try:
- arancia (vowel start, fem.) → **l'arancia** ✅
- chiave, plural → **le chiavi** ✅
- amico, plural → **gli amici** ✅

### REFLECT
Asked the learner to explain why "gli" exists as a separate category and what it inherits from the singular.
Answer: "gli herda o comportamento 'fora do padrão' de lo e l'. e ele existe para dar uma fluidez fonetica"
Correct and complete — learner identified both the inheritance mechanism and the phonetic-collision rationale unprompted. No re-teaching needed.
